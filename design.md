# cryptik — Design System

> Brand spec for the cryptik landing page. Source of truth for color, type, and usage. Build the page only after this is settled.

**cryptik** — spacetech company · `cryptik.tech`

---

## Palette

Four colors. No others.

| Token | Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--blue` | cryptik blue | `#2B5BE0` | `43, 91, 224` | **Primary** |
| `--red` | cryptik red | `#E63946` | `230, 57, 70` | Accent only |
| `--cream` | cryptik cream | `#F5ECD7` | `245, 236, 215` | Text on dark |
| `--black` | cryptik black | `#0B0B0F` | `11, 11, 15` | Canvas |

- Black is the canvas; cream is text on dark.
- Blue is primary. Red is accent — never a primary fill or background.

## Type

| Use | Font | Weight | Size | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headings, logo wordmark | **Space Grotesk** | Bold 700 | 28–48px | `-0.02em` |
| Body, captions, UI text | **Inter** | Regular 400 | 10–16px | `-0.01em` |

That's it. No other typefaces.

## Logo

Three locked elements that move together: **planetary dome · orbital arc · constellation**. Always keep them locked; maintain clear space around the mark.

## Rules

**Do**
- Use the four brand colors only.
- Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for body.
- Cream text on dark, dark text on light.
- Maintain clear space around the logo.
- Use official logo files, never recreate.

**Don't**
- Stretch, rotate, or distort the logo.
- Add shadows, glows, gradients, or effects.
- Use red as a primary fill or background.
- Introduce additional typefaces.
- Place the logo on low-contrast backgrounds.

## Voice

Lowercase, terse, confident. Short declarative lines ("that's it.", "no others.", "follow it."). Match the press-kit tone.
